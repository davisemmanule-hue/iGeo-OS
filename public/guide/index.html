1Getting Started

Open iGeo OS

  1. Go to igeosolutionsllc.com.
  2. The Today tab and Operator Dashboard open first.

Desktop app

  1. Open your installed iGeo shortcut or browser bookmark.
  2. If the browser asks, allow the site to open.
  3. Use the top tabs to move between modules; the blue tab is the active module.

iPhone Home Screen app

  1. Tap the iGeo icon on the Home Screen.
  2. If it is not installed, open the site in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Today to return to the dashboard.

Search and navigation

  1. Tap or click Search iGeo OS at the top.
  2. Enter a company, opportunity, worker, registration, NAICS, agency, or module name.
  3. Select a result. Clear the search to start again.
  4. Use Today, Acquisition OS, Contacts, Quotes, Applications, Registrations, Capability Statements, or Settings. A blue tab shows where you are.
  5. Inside Acquisition OS, click Return to Operator Dashboard to return to Today.

Cloud data and status

  1. In a module with a refresh control, click Refresh Opportunity Data or the visible refresh/sync control.
  2. Wait for the status and “Last Synced” time to update before editing.

Cloud Synced / Synced
The latest save or refresh reached the shared source.

Offline Local Backup / Offline Backup
Your browser kept a local copy; another device may not see it yet.

Sync Failed
The cloud request failed. Keep the page open, check the internet, then retry refresh. Export critical records before clearing browser data.

2Today Dashboard

Morning routine

  1. Open Today.
  2. Review the top cards: urgent emails, follow-ups, applications, opportunities, bid items, registrations, quotes, and contracts.
  3. Click My Day Checklist to open it. Check items as you work; click the heading again to close it.
  4. Review Alerts — Needs Attention for contracts, payments, deadlines, applications, SAM, and registrations.
  5. Open the highest-priority module from the blue top tabs.

Operator Settings

  1. Click Settings in the top navigation.
  2. Review Partner Notifications, Email Alerts, Simple Mode, Advanced Mode, Partner View, and Automation Status.
  3. Click Today to close Settings and return. Settings are saved in this browser.
3Acquisition OS
  1. Click Acquisition OS.
  2. Click Opportunity for quick entry.
  3. Enter the required Opportunity Name; then add every known field: source/link, solicitation type/number, buyer/agency, service, NAICS, region, due date, value, performance method, decision, contact, notes, and score flags.
  4. Review the form and click Save.
  5. To edit, find the row and click its edit control, change the fields, and save again.
  6. To remove a record, use the row’s delete/archive control, verify the correct record, and confirm. Export first if it must be retained.
  7. Click CSV Export to download the visible records.
  8. Click Refresh Opportunity Data to reload canonical records.
  9. Click Return to Operator Dashboard for Today.
  10. Click Open Full Bid Engine; it opens in a separate tab.
4Full Bid Engine — Complete Workflow
Before you begin: Refresh opportunity data, select the correct opportunity, and save after each major step. Generated content and all pricing must be manually reviewed and approved.

Step 1 — Opportunity Dashboard

  1. Open Acquisition OS, then Open Full Bid Engine.
  2. In Opportunity Pipeline, search for and select the opportunity. If it does not exist, click New, enter the core record, then Save.
  3. Open Opportunity Dashboard. Confirm agency/buyer, solicitation number, due date, NAICS, source link, and service type against the solicitation.

Step 2 — Solicitation Analyzer

  1. Click Solicitation Analyzer.
  2. Paste the solicitation text into the intake/analyzer field. If an upload control is shown, choose the solicitation file; otherwise paste its relevant text.
  3. Review scope, deadlines, required documents, and submission instructions.
  4. Correct incomplete or inaccurate extracted fields against the source document, then click Save.

Step 3 — Opportunity Scoring Engine

  1. Click Opportunity Scoring Engine.
  2. Review the fit/score fields and every risk flag.
  3. Check startup-capital or low-capital concerns and whether work is subcontractable.
  4. Review the go/no-go decision and reasons. Do not rely on the score alone; set the decision only after owner review.
  5. Click Save.

Step 4 — Compliance Checklist

  1. Click Compliance Checklist Generator.
  2. Generate or populate the checklist from the opportunity.
  3. Verify required forms, certifications, insurance, bonding/licenses, and submission attachments against the solicitation.
  4. Mark an item complete only when its final version is ready, then Save.

Step 5 — Proposal Draft

  1. Click Proposal Draft Generator and generate the draft.
  2. Review company information, technical approach, management approach, staffing, and past performance.
  3. Replace placeholders, correct assumptions, and remove unsupported claims.
  4. Click Save.

Step 6 — Subcontractor / Teaming Review

  1. Click Subcontractor / Teaming Partner Tracker.
  2. Use scope, licenses, location, capacity, and score flags to decide whether a partner is needed.
  3. Add partner name, contact information, capability/services, license status, relationship status, and next action.
  4. Confirm availability directly with the partner, then Save.

Step 7 — Pricing Worksheet

  1. Click Pricing Worksheet.
  2. Enter labor quantities/rates, supplies or materials, subcontractor/partner costs, overhead, and profit.
  3. Review the calculated final price against the solicitation format and all source estimates.
  4. Manually review and approve pricing. The worksheet is decision support, not automatic approval.
  5. Click Save.

Step 8 — Final Proposal Package

  1. Review every module and compliance item.
  2. Confirm names, dates, factual assumptions, representations, attachments, and approved pricing.
  3. Click Word to download the Word-compatible proposal. Open it and inspect formatting.
  4. Click PDF; in the browser print window choose Save as PDF. Open and inspect the saved PDF.
  5. Save final files in the approved opportunity folder, confirm file names and submission instructions, then mark the opportunity ready to submit.

Step 9 — Submission Tracking

  1. After submission, return to the active opportunity.
  2. Record the submission date, portal/email confirmation number, follow-up date, and buyer/contact details in the available notes/contact fields.
  3. Update the opportunity status/decision label to the correct submitted stage and click Save.
5Complete Procurement Process
  1. Open iGeo OS, click Acquisition OS, then Open Full Bid Engine.
  2. Click Import Solicitation. Paste a source link or solicitation text, or upload a PDF, DOCX, or TXT file.
  3. Click Extract and Review. Correct every field, fill missing information, confirm verification, and click Save as New Opportunity. If extraction is unavailable, preserve the link and complete manual review.
  4. Click Build Proposal Workspace. Complete every section marked Operator Review Required.
  5. Use the existing Analyzer, Scoring, Compliance, Proposal, Pricing, and Teaming tools. Approve only verified pricing, staffing, partner, insurance, certification, and past-performance statements.
  6. Open Procurement Timeline to review the current stage and record operator notes or blockers.
  7. Open Procurement Assistant for rule-based next actions, missing items, deadline, and readiness.
  8. Open Final Submission Review and complete all confirmations after verification.
  9. Use Open Submission Portal, copy the solicitation number, and download the Word or PDF package. iGeo OS does not submit to outside portals.
  10. After manual submission, record its date, time, confirmation number, submitter, method, notes, and follow-up date, then save.
6Subcontractor Network
  1. Open Acquisition OS, then Open Full Bid Engine and select Subcontractor Network.
  2. Use Search and the service or region filters to find prospects. The initial 25 numbered records are research placeholders, not verified companies.
  3. Click Add Subcontractor, complete the intake fields, choose services, qualification, document checklist, and responsibility acknowledgements, then save.
  4. Use Edit to update a record or Delete only after confirming the correct record.
  5. Click Match to compare a subcontractor with saved Acquisition OS opportunities. Review service, location, capacity, equipment, insurance, licenses, availability, experience, and documents. Saving a match does not promise or assign work.
  6. Click CSV Export to download the directory. All network records persist in this browser.
  7. Review iGeo Contract Control: iGeo retains buyer communication, pricing, submission, selection, scope, quality, payment, compliance, and customer control.
7Prime Contractor CRM
  1. Click Contacts.
  2. Click Contact, add company and primary/small-business contact information, relationship status, services, opportunity notes, last contact, and next follow-up date; then save.
  3. To edit, find the company and click its edit control. Update and save.
  4. Use Search Companies or the status, service, follow-up, and sort filters.
  5. To archive, open the correct record, use its delete/archive control, and confirm. The cloud workflow treats removal as an archive where available.
  6. Use the visible cloud refresh/recovery control when present, or reload Contacts and wait for the shared records to load.

CRM saves locally first and then attempts Google Sheets sync. A failed save is queued for retry. For laptop/iPhone consistency, use the same live site, wait for cloud completion, then refresh on the other device.

6Workforce Management
  1. Click Applications.
  2. Review the worker table. Click Worker Intake Form to view the public application path in a new tab.
  3. Click Add Worker, enter identity/contact details, service, location, availability, rate, driver-license/vehicle status, background-check status, and notes; then save.
  4. To edit, select the worker’s edit control, update availability or credentials, and save.
  5. Search by name or use service, city, state, and status filters.
  6. Before deleting/archiving, confirm the worker and export if retention is required. Use the row control and confirm.
  7. Reload the module to refresh intake data from Google Sheets when connected.

Public intake rows can come from Google Sheets. Manually added Workforce records may remain local to the browser, so verify the other device before assuming sync.

7Gmail Alerts

Unread business-critical email can trigger dashboard alerts when the Gmail connection is healthy. Contract/solicitation/award terms map to contract urgency; payment/invoice terms to payments; workforce/application terms to applications; SAM/CAGE and vendor terms should be reviewed under their related alerts.

iGeo - Urgent
Immediate deadlines or critical action.

iGeo - Contracts
Solicitations, proposals, awards, primes, and government contracts.

iGeo - Payments
Invoices, deposits, payments, and wire-transfer notices.

iGeo - Workforce
Worker applications and staffing.

iGeo - SAM CAGE
SAM.gov, entity, or CAGE notices.

Promotions, shopping, social, routine marketing, subscriptions, newsletters, webinars, and coupons normally stay silent unless they also contain a critical term. Owner notifications go to the configured owner email; the second owner/partner receives them only when Partner Notifications is on and the partner email is configured.

If notifications stop

  1. Open Settings and confirm Email Alerts and Partner Notifications.
  2. Check Gmail directly and the configured iGeo label.
  3. Refresh Today and check Automation Status.
  4. If counts remain blank or fail, ask the system administrator to verify the Gmail connection/credentials. Do not change Cloudflare settings from this guide.
8Vendor Registration Tracker
  1. Click Registrations, then Add Registration.
  2. Add company/vendor portal, website, portal type, login email/username, status, submission date, follow-up date, renewal details in Notes, contact, capability-statement status, and notes.
  3. Save the record.
  4. Use Search Registrations and the status filter.
  5. Use the record’s edit control to update it; save again.
  6. Use the delete/archive control only after confirming the record and exporting anything needed.

Live locally; cloud sync coming soon. The tracker is available, but its Google Sheets sync is not active. Records may be browser-specific.

9Capability Statements
  1. Click Capability Statements.
  2. Use the Master statement for a general company introduction.
  3. Use Commercial Cleaning for janitorial/facilities opportunities; Workforce for staffing/labor support; and AI Automation for workflow or technology services.
  4. Choose the version matching the buyer’s need. Open/download it from its card, or use its copy/email action if configured.
  5. Review the file before sending, attach it in Gmail, address the intended contact, and send.
  6. Click Mark Sent only after sending.

The library card links point to the configured storage location. If a file does not open, verify its configured link or find the approved master in Google Drive. “Mark Sent” updates its counter but does not create a CRM activity automatically.

10Troubleshooting
Laptop and iPhone show different data
Confirm both use the live URL. Save and wait for Synced, refresh the other device, and remember that local-only modules do not automatically cross devices.
App opens old data
Refresh the module/page. On iPhone, fully close and reopen the Home Screen app. Do not clear site data until critical local records are exported.
Search does not update
Clear the search, refresh data, then enter the term again.
Email notifications stop
Check Settings, Gmail directly, Automation Status, and ask the administrator to verify the Gmail connection.
Cloud Sync Failed
Check internet access, preserve the local tab, retry refresh/save, and export critical records.
Full Bid Engine does not open
Allow pop-ups/new tabs for the site, or use the Full Bid Engine Quick Link below.
Word export fails
Save first, select an opportunity, retry Word, and allow downloads.
PDF export fails
Select Proposal Draft, retry PDF, then choose Save as PDF in the browser print screen.
Page opens scrolled down
Tap the iGeo logo or Today; for this guide, tap the section in the contents menu.
Mobile buttons missing
Scroll horizontally only inside tables, rotate the phone if needed, close the keyboard, and reload. Use Safari at normal zoom.
